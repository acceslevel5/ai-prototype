BOG UI

Progress Tracker

Open in Figma ↗

The steps of a longer process, top to bottom — a loan application, a card delivery. Each step is completed, current, pending, or needs attention (warning, error) with a message. The line under a completed step turns green.

Usage

<ol class="bog-tracker">                                     <!-- data-state="skeleton" -->
  <li class="bog-tracker__step" data-state="completed">
    <span class="bog-tracker__indicator"></span>
    <div class="bog-tracker__content">
      <p class="bog-tracker__title">Application sent</p>
      <p class="bog-tracker__helper">3 Nov, 10:42</p>
      <p class="bog-tracker__desc">We received your documents</p>
    </div>
  </li>
  <li class="bog-tracker__step" data-state="current">…</li>
  <li class="bog-tracker__step"><span class="bog-tracker__indicator" data-number="3"></span>…</li>
</ol>
