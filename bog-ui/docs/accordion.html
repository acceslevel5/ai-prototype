BOG UI

Accordion

Open in Figma ↗

Groups of detail that users open when they need them — fees, terms, the parts of a statement. Each header is a row with a title (one to three lines), an optional icon on the left and a value on the right; the chevron turns and the content opens under it, pushing the rest down. Single mode keeps one open at a time. In progress in Figma.

Usage

<div class="bog-accordion">                          <!-- data-single: one open at a time -->
  <details class="bog-accordion__item">
    <summary class="bog-accordion__header">
      <span class="bog-accordion__titles"><span class="bog-accordion__title">Fees and limits</span></span>
      <span class="bog-accordion__value">12.00 ₾</span>   <!-- optional -->
    </summary>
    <div class="bog-accordion__content"><p>…</p></div>
  </details>
</div>
