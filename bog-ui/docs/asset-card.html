BOG UI

Asset Card

Open in Figma ↗

One holding in an investment portfolio: its ticker and name, today's change and its value. The change is green with an up arrow when positive, red with a down arrow when negative, grey with no arrow when flat. Cards sit in a row that scrolls sideways and load in one after another — from the left, the right, below or above — with a small delay between cards. Try it in the phone on the right.
