BOG UI

Tour Popover

Open in Figma ↗

One step of an onboarding tour: a white card with an arrow pointing at the part of the screen it explains. A title, one or two lines of description, the step count, Skip and Next. The arrow sits on the top or bottom edge, at the left, centre or right, so it can point at its target.

Usage

<div class="bog-tour" data-position="bottom" data-align="center" role="dialog" aria-label="Tip 1 of 3">
  <div class="bog-tour__head">
    <div class="bog-tour__text"><p class="bog-tour__title">Your cards</p><p class="bog-tour__desc">Swipe to see all of them.</p></div>
    <button class="bog-icon-btn" data-type="neutral" data-size="small" aria-label="Close tour"><span class="bog-icon" data-icon="close"></span></button>
  </div>
  <div class="bog-tour__foot">
    <span class="bog-tour__count">1 / 3</span>
    <button class="bog-btn" data-type="text-primary" data-size="small">Skip</button>
    <button class="bog-btn" data-type="primary" data-size="small">Next</button>
  </div>
</div>

Try it

Next walks through three steps; the popover moves to point at each part.
