Navigation Bar

Open in Figma ↗

The bar at the top of a screen: back button, title, and up to two actions on the right. The title can carry secondary text below or a caption above. Themed follows the app theme; White and Black Static keep fixed colours over imagery. The status bar is included for mockups.
