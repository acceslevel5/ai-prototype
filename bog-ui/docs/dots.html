BOG UI

Slider Indicator

Open in Figma ↗

Page dots under a carousel or an onboarding pager. Up to five pages show every dot; from six up, a window of five slides along and the dots at its edges shrink to say there are more. White appearance over imagery.

Usage

<div class="bog-dots" data-count="8" data-current="1" aria-label="Slides"></div>
<script> BOG.dots(el, 3); /* move to page 3 */ </script>
