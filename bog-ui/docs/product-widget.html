BOG UI

Product Widget

Open in Figma ↗

The home screen's product tiles — accounts and cards, deposits, loans — two to a row. Each shows the product's picture, its name and the balance; an expired product shows a red badge instead. With an odd number of tiles the last one spans the row with the picture on the right. In progress in Figma.
