BOG UI

Wheel Picker

Open in Figma ↗

Picks one value from an ordered list by scrolling — a loan term, a day, an hour. The option in the middle is selected, large and dark; the ones around it get smaller and fainter. Multi puts several wheels side by side, like day · month · year.

Usage

<div class="bog-wheel" role="listbox" aria-label="Term" tabindex="0">
  <div class="bog-wheel__option" role="option">3 month</div>
  <div class="bog-wheel__option" role="option" aria-selected="true">5 month</div>
  …
</div>
<!-- Multi: <div class="bog-wheel-group"> two or three .bog-wheel </div> -->
