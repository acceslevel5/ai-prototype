Charts for spending and goals: Donut Chart (Large and Mini), Card Chart and Sparkline. bog-ui.js draws them from a list of numbers. Give the donut a target and it shows how far off it is — a dashed outline for what's missing, a hatched red part and a tick for what's over.

Usage

<div class="bog-donut" data-values="25,16,3,3,13,12,24" data-selectable>   <!-- data-size="mini" · data-max · data-colors · data-labels -->
  <div class="bog-donut__center"><span class="bog-donut__label">Spent</span><span class="bog-donut__value">124.00 ₾</span></div>
</div>
<svg class="bog-sparkline" data-values="3,5,5,8,10,9,7,9,4,2,1"></svg>
<script> el.dataset.values = "…"; BOG.donut(el); </script>
