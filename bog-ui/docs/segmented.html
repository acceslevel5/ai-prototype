BOG UI

Segmented Control

Open in Figma ↗

Switch between a few views of the same content in place — a period, a category or a list/grid view. Large holds two to five segments, Small up to six (like a chart's periods). Icon only: two universally known icons, each with an accessible name. A different kind of content → Tabs.

Usage

<div class="bog-segmented" role="radiogroup" aria-label="Period">        <!-- data-size="small" · data-state="skeleton" -->
  <button class="bog-segment" role="radio" aria-checked="true">24 h</button>
  <button class="bog-segment" role="radio" aria-checked="false">Week</button>
  <button class="bog-segment" role="radio" aria-checked="false">All</button>
</div>

<div class="bog-segmented" data-type="icon" role="radiogroup" aria-label="View">
  <button class="bog-segment" role="radio" aria-checked="true" aria-label="List view" data-icon="list-view"></button>
  <button class="bog-segment" role="radio" aria-checked="false" aria-label="Grid view" data-icon="dashboard"></button>
</div>
