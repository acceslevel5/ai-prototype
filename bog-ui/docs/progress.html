BOG UI

Progress Bar

Open in Figma ↗

How far something has come toward a known end — a savings goal, a spending limit or the steps of a short flow. Single fills one bar; Segmented splits it into 2–10 equal steps. Colour carries the status: Accent while in progress, Positive when a goal is reached, Warning near a limit, Negative at the limit, Neutral with no status. Labels give the numbers. Read-only.

Usage

<div class="bog-progress" data-size="thick" style="--value: 48%" role="progressbar" aria-valuenow="48">
  <div class="bog-progress__track"><span class="bog-progress__fill"></span></div>
  <div class="bog-progress__text">
    <div><span class="bog-progress__label">2,400.00 ₾</span><span class="bog-progress__helper">Saved</span></div>
    <div><span class="bog-progress__label">5,000.00 ₾</span><span class="bog-progress__helper">Goal</span></div>
  </div>
</div>

<!-- Segmented: one span per step, data-done on the reached ones -->
<div class="bog-progress" data-progress="segmented">
  <div class="bog-progress__track"><span data-done></span><span data-done></span><span></span><span></span></div>
</div>
