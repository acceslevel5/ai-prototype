BOG UI

Summary

Open in Figma ↗

A card of the details users check before they confirm a transfer or a payment: each row a title and a value, with optional secondary text, a helper message and a slot on the right for an info icon, a flag or a card. Helper messages show a problem (negative), something to check (warning) or good news (positive).

Usage

<dl class="bog-summary">                                      <!-- data-state="skeleton" -->
  <div class="bog-summary__item">
    <dt class="bog-summary__title">Transfer fee</dt>
    <dd class="bog-summary__value">1.00 ₾</dd>
    <dd class="bog-summary__secondary">Charged by the receiving bank</dd>     <!-- optional -->
    <dd class="bog-summary__helper"><span class="bog-inline-message" data-size="small" data-status="negative">…</span></dd>
    <dd class="bog-summary__slot">…tooltip, flag or card…</dd>             <!-- optional -->
  </div>
</dl>
