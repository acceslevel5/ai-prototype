Keyboard Numeric

Open in Figma ↗

The in-app number pad for PIN and amount entry: 1–9, 0, delete, and one extra key — Face ID, fingerprint, a decimal dot, or empty. Keys are 56 px tall and show a grey pad when pressed. Themed, White Static, Black Static and Invert.

Usage

<div class="bog-keypad" data-for="amount">              <!-- data-appearance="white | black | invert" -->
  <button class="bog-keypad__key" value="1">1</button> … <button class="bog-keypad__key" value="9">9</button>
  <button class="bog-keypad__key" data-key="dot" aria-label="Decimal point"></button>   <!-- face-id · fingerprint · dot, or <span></span> -->
  <button class="bog-keypad__key" value="0">0</button>
  <button class="bog-keypad__key" data-key="backspace" aria-label="Delete"></button>
</div>
